    Who We Are

    About Jeeva Bricks & Metals

    Jeeva Bricks & Metals is a stone crushing and building materials unit at Vadakke Poiloor, Thrippangottur, in Kannur district. We produce and supply manufactured sand, plastering sand, crushed metal, quarry dust, road base materials and building blocks for residential, commercial and road works.

    With our own crushing and screening line, we control quality from rock to finished product — so what reaches your site is clean, correctly graded and ready to use.

    To be the most trusted source of building materials in North Kerala — known for consistent quality, honest measure and dependable delivery.

    To supply properly graded, tested materials at fair prices, deliver on the day we promise, and support sustainable construction by offering M-Sand as a reliable alternative to river sand.

    • Own crushing & screening plant
    • Consistent grading for every load
    • Tipper delivery across Kannur district
    • Supply for homes, builders & road works

    How It Works

    From Rock To Your Site

    1. 01

      Quarrying

      Hard granite rock is selected and fed into the plant.

    2. 02

      Crushing

      Multi-stage crushers break rock down to the required sizes.

    3. 03

      Screening

      Vibrating screens separate sand and metal into precise grades.

    4. 04

      Delivery

      Measured loads are dispatched by tipper straight to your site.

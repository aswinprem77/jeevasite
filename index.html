    Who We Are

    About Jeeva Bricks & Metals

    Jeeva Bricks & Metals is a stone crushing unit at Vadakke Poiloor, Thrippangottur, in Kannur district. We produce and supply manufactured sand, plastering sand, crushed metal, quarry dust and road base materials for residential, commercial and road works.

    With our own crushing and screening line, we control quality from rock to finished product — so what reaches your site is clean, correctly graded and ready to use.

    To be the most trusted source of crushed stone and sand in North Kerala — known for consistent quality, honest measure and dependable delivery.

    To supply properly graded materials at fair prices, deliver on the day we promise, and support sustainable construction by offering M-Sand as a reliable alternative to river sand.

    • Own crushing & screening plant
    • Consistent grading for every load
    • Tipper delivery across Kannur
    • Homes, builders & road works
